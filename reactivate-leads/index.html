Questions

Before you ask.

What do I need to start?

A list of past leads with names, phone numbers and, ideally, what each person asked about and when. A CSV or spreadsheet is enough.

Which channel does it use?

WhatsApp, using approved message templates for the first message, as WhatsApp requires for messages a business starts.

Isn’t this just automated spam?

It would be if it sent the same message to everyone. Each message refers to what that person asked about, goes only to people who agreed to hear from you, and stops as soon as someone says so. Warm replies go to a person on your team.

Do you guarantee results?

No. Results depend on the list. That is why the pilot comes first, and why you see your own numbers before any payment.

What happens to my list?

It is used only for your pilot and deleted when the pilot ends.

What if my list is small?

With fewer than a couple of hundred contacts, results are hard to measure and new leads may matter more. We would rather tell you that than start a pilot that cannot show you anything.

What happens after the pilot?

If it worked, we agree terms together, based on the leads and booked calls it produces. If it did not, we stop and you owe nothing.

Does it speak Arabic?

Yes. Kai replies in the language the person writes in, including English and Arabic.

Is it legal to contact old leads?

It depends on whether those people agreed to receive messages from you. We ask about this before every pilot and only work with lists that have consent. If you are unsure about your list, check with a lawyer first.
