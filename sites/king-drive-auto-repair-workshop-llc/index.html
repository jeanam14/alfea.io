Before you book

Do you give a quote before starting?

Yes. We explain what we found and what it will cost before any repair is agreed.

Can I bring my own parts?

Ask us when you call and we will tell you what works for your job.

How long does a standard service take?

It depends on the car and the checks needed. We give you an estimate when you book.
