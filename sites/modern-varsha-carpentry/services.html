Modern Custom Furniture

Dining tables, TV units, consoles, beds and study desks designed around your room rather than a catalogue size. We work from your measurements and references, and finish in veneer, lacquer or solid wood to suit the rest of your interior. Every piece is built in our Al Quoz workshop before delivery and assembly.

  • TV and media units
  • Dining and coffee tables
  • Beds and headboards
  • Home-office desks

Signs you may need this

  • Catalogue sizes do not fit your room
  • You want a piece to match your interior
  • You have a reference image to build from

Wardrobes & Built-ins

Fitted wardrobes, walk-in closets, under-stair storage and alcove shelving that use every centimetre of wall height. Interiors are planned around what you actually store, with hanging, drawers and shoe racks. Sliding, hinged or handleless fronts, fitted cleanly to uneven walls.

  • Floor-to-ceiling wardrobes
  • Walk-in closets
  • Kids' room storage
  • Under-stair cabinets

  • You are fitting out a new bedroom

Doors & Partitions

Interior doors, feature entrance doors, sliding and pocket doors, and timber or timber-and-glass partitions to divide open-plan spaces. We supply, hang and align frames, hinges and locks so everything closes properly. Ideal for villa upgrades, office fit-outs and turning one room into two.

  • Interior and entrance doors
  • Sliding and pocket doors
  • Office partitions
  • Frame and lock replacement

Signs you may need this

  • Doors drop, stick or will not lock
  • You want to divide an open plan
  • You are upgrading a villa or office

Repairs & Refinishing

Sagging cabinet doors, broken hinges, swollen or scratched woodwork, loose stair parts and worn finishes, repaired rather than replaced. We sand, fill, re-stain or re-lacquer to bring tired surfaces back. A practical option before a move-out inspection or a home refresh.

  • Hinge and runner replacement
  • Door re-alignment
  • Scratch and water-damage repair
  • Sanding and re-lacquering

Signs you may need this

  • Doors sag or scrape
  • Surfaces are scratched or stained
  • You are preparing a move-out
