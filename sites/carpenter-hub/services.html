Repairs & Refinishing

Sticking doors, loose hinges, damaged cabinet fronts, worn or sun-faded wood: we repair what can be saved instead of replacing it. Sanding, filling, re-staining and lacquering bring tired woodwork back to a clean, even finish. It is often the quickest and most cost-effective upgrade for a home.

  • Hinge and handle replacement
  • Door planing and re-hanging
  • Cabinet front repair
  • Sanding and re-lacquering
  • Sun and water damage touch-up

Signs you may need this

  • Finish is faded or scratched
  • Hardware is loose or broken
  • You want a refresh without replacing
